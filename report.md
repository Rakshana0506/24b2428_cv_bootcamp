# CV Selection Bootcamp: CIFAR-10N report

Q1. The noise sits in a few look-alike pairs. Automobile/truck alone is 13.1% of all wrong labels (truck labelled automobile 1,247 times, automobile labelled truck 1,126), then cat/dog 9.7%, deer/horse 5.1% and airplane/ship 4.1%. These four pairs make up 32% of the 18,109 wrong labels. By true class, cat (51%) and deer (51%) are mislabelled most and horse (28%) least. The synthetic labels have about the same overall rate, but no single pair takes more than 2.4% of their errors.

Q2. Around epoch 31. Up to about epoch 30 the network predicts only 12 to 14% of the mislabelled training images as their wrong label, while about 65% are still predicted as their true class. After that the fit to wrong labels climbs steadily to 68% at epoch 60, and the share predicted as the true class falls to 22%. Clean validation accuracy peaks near 78% and ends at 68.4%. The climb starts as the cosine learning rate gets small. Noisy validation accuracy peaks at epoch 38, used for early stopping.

Q3. Cross validated confidence over 5 folds, averaged from epoch 10 to each fold's best noisy validation epoch: AUROC 0.942, precision@k 0.831. Early loss from the Task 1 run was close (0.937), but using only the last fold epoch fell to 0.877, because by then the fold models copy the annotators' mistakes. Failure mode: systematic confusions. Only 36% of trucks labelled automobile reach the top k, against over 90% for rarer errors like deer labelled dog, since the model learns the same truck/car confusion as the humans.

Q4. Co-teaching was best: noisy validation accuracy 0.579 and clean validation accuracy 85.1%, against 68.4% for plain cross entropy and 77.9% with early stopping. Each network updates only on the small loss part of every batch chosen by the other, so wrong labels, which keep a high loss early on, are mostly skipped, and the two networks do not reinforce each other's errors. Its accuracy kept rising until epoch 60 with no memorisation dip. Relabelling with the Task 3 scores came close (84.1%) after cutting label noise from 40% to 7%.
